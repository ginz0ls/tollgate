### Task 11: I cinque workflow e l'installazione

**Files:**
- Create: `gate/workflows/tollgate-guard.yml`, `gate/workflows/tollgate-run.yml`, `gate/workflows/tollgate-verdict.yml`, `gate/workflows/tollgate-base.yml`, `gate/workflows/tollgate-sentinel.yml`, `bin/tollgate_gate/install.py`, `tests/test_gate_workflows.py`

**Interfaces:**
- Consumes: `prereq.GATE_FILES` (i nomi **esatti**), i sottocomandi dei Task 7-10.
- Produces in `install.py`: `WORKFLOWS_DIR = "gate/workflows"`; `fingerprint(bin_root: Path) -> str`; `files(plugin_root: Path, *, default_branch: str, services: dict[str, str]) -> dict[str, bytes]` (percorso nel repo del progetto → contenuto: i cinque workflow, `.github/tollgate/VERSION`, `.github/tollgate/FINGERPRINT`, `.github/tollgate/bin/**` senza `__pycache__`), servizi inseriti nel job `head` di `tollgate-run`.

Contenuto dei workflow (9.1, decisioni 5, 12, 16, 17):
- `tollgate-guard`: `on: pull_request_target` (tipi `opened`, `synchronize`, `reopened`, `edited`); `permissions: {contents: read, statuses: write, pull-requests: write}`; checkout con `ref: ${{ github.event.repository.default_branch }}`, `persist-credentials: false`, `fetch-depth: 0`; `env: GH_TOKEN: ${{ github.token }}`; `gate guard --event "$GITHUB_EVENT_PATH" --publish`.
- `tollgate-run`: `on: pull_request` (tipi `opened`, `synchronize`, `reopened`, `edited`; nessun `paths` né `branches`); `permissions: {contents: read}`; `concurrency: {group: tollgate-run-${{ github.event.pull_request.number }}, cancel-in-progress: true}`; job `head`: checkout di `github.event.pull_request.head.sha` (in `with:`), `fetch-depth: 0`, `persist-credentials: false`, `setup` dalla configurazione del predefinito, `gate run`, `upload-artifact` `tollgate-run`; job `base`: `actions/cache/restore` di `tollgate-ids-<albero di merge-base>` (chiave calcolata da un passo che scrive in `$GITHUB_OUTPUT`), solo se manca checkout della base con `persist-credentials: false`, `gate base-ids`, upload `tollgate-base-ids`.
- `tollgate-verdict`: `on: workflow_run: {workflows: [tollgate-run], types: [completed]}` (anche con il run cancellato o fallito: niente `if:` sulla conclusione); `permissions: {contents: read, statuses: write, pull-requests: write, actions: read, issues: read}`; checkout con `ref: ${{ github.event.repository.default_branch }}` e `persist-credentials: false`; `download-artifact` di `tollgate-run` e di `tollgate-base-ids` con `run-id`, `github-token`, `path:` sotto `${{ runner.temp }}` e `continue-on-error: true`; `actions/cache/restore` della base con `path:` sotto `${{ runner.temp }}`; `gate evaluate … --publish --head-ids-out` (`env: GH_TOKEN`); `actions/cache/save` solo se il file esiste.
- `tollgate-base`: `on: push` sul ramo `__DEFAULT_BRANCH__` (lo sostituisce `install.files`); `permissions: {contents: read, statuses: write, issues: write}`; checkout del predefinito con `persist-credentials: false`; cache dell'albero; `gate base-ids --publish` (in `detect`); `actions/cache/save`.
- `tollgate-sentinel`: `on: push` sul ramo `__DEFAULT_BRANCH__`; `permissions: {contents: read, statuses: write, issues: write, pull-requests: read}`; checkout `fetch-depth: 0`; `gate sentinel --event "$GITHUB_EVENT_PATH" --publish`.
